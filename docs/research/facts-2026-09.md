# Verified facts — tax, law, devices, platforms (checked 24–25 Sep 2026)

Every fact comes from a page that was opened; "not confirmed" says what is missing. Re-check any fact older than six
months before relying on it for a client.

## A. Tax and law
1. **VAT on restaurant and café sales.** 10 % for on-site food and drink (Upsilon 2026 table; Sahl Compta, Mar 2026; the
   DGI return's line 111 "vente de denrées ou de boissons à consommer sur place, réalisées dans les restaurants" on an older
   form). Standard 20 %. The 2026 Finance Law (loi 50-25) changed nothing for restaurants (Deloitte, 7 Apr 2026). Takeaway
   and delivery: **not confirmed** (a DGI answer of 16/06/2015 exists behind a paywall).
   [Upsilon](https://www.upsilon-consulting.com/en/vat-in-morocco/) · [Sahl Compta](https://sahlcompta.ma/blog/tva-maroc-2026) · [DGI form](https://rabat.eregulations.org/media/Formulaire%20de%20d%C3%A9claration%20de%20la%20TVA.pdf) · [Deloitte](https://blog.avocats.deloitte.fr/maroc-les-principales-mesures-de-la-loi-de-finances-pour-2026/)
2. **E-invoicing.** CGI art. 145-IX (Finance Law 2018): "doivent se doter d'un système informatique de facturation qui répond
   aux critères techniques déterminés par l'administration" (as quoted by Sage). Decree drafted and sent to the SGG; launch
   "during 2026", phased (Médias24, 16 and 18 Apr 2026). Clearance model, UBL, qualified signature, free portal for small
   firms (Sage, 14 May 2026). B2B first; B2C later; retail and restaurants not planned at this stage; phases reported by
   Le360 (3 Jun 2026): 2026 ≈1,655 companies > MAD 200 m, 2027–2028 SMEs, after 2028 very small companies. No published
   decree found. No certification scheme for POS software found.
   [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/04/18/la-facturation-electronique-expliquee-par-younes-idrissi-kaitouni-directeur-des-impots-1661379/) · [Sage](https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/) · [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/facturation-electronique-le-detail-du-calendrier-de-mise-en-place_2EQSNG2DTVB6PGBBHQ6OCFT6CY/)
3. **Receipt obligation.** Law 31-08 art. 4: "Le fournisseur est tenu de délivrer une facture, quittance, ticket de caisse ou
   tout autre document en tenant lieu." Invoice mentions per secondary sources (ClicPaie, 13 May 2026; Fawatir, 19 Jul 2026);
   buyer's ICE: a 2019 DGI note says not mandatory, ClicPaie says mandatory in B2B — **not confirmed**.
   [consommateurs.ma](https://www.consommateurs.ma/accueil/droit/loi_31_08) · [ClicPaie](https://clicpaie.ma/blogs/modele-facture-maroc/)
4. **Débit-de-boissons tax.** Law 47-06 art. 64–67: cafés, bars, tea rooms; base = drinks receipts HT; 2–10 % by commune;
   declaration within 30 days then yearly before 1 April; quarterly payment. Casablanca 8 % (penalty 15 % + 0.5 %/month, min
   MAD 500); Salé 5 %.
   [Law 47-06](https://rabat.eregulations.org/media/Loi%2047-06%20relative%20aux%20collectivit%C3%A9s%20locales.pdf) · [Casablanca](https://www.casablancacity.ma/fr/article/285/taxe-sur-debit-de-boissons) · [Salé](https://fr.villedesale.ma/taxe-sur-les-debits-de-boissons/)
5. **Law 09-08 (CNDP).** Consent, information, objection, declaration before processing, security, transfers abroad only
   with adequate protection or an exception. Customer management: simplified declaration F-214 (deliberation 32-2015);
   transfers: F-118; HR processing needs prior authorisation (CNDP procedures page, 27 Jul 2026). Penalties MAD
   10,000–200,000 and prison for some breaches. Biometrics: deliberation 478-2013 — "La gestion du temps de présence des
   fonctionnaires et des employés ne peut être retenue comme finalité".
   [Law](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf) · [procedures](https://www.cndp.ma/procedures-de-notification-process/) · [478-2013](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/deliberation-n-478-2013-01-11-2013.pdf)
6. **Price display.** Law 31-08 art. 3 and 5: prices shown TTC, including mandatory service charges; decree of 2013: visible
   and readable, in dirhams. [fiche](https://almostahlik.ma/pdf/Fiche%20Pratique%20Affichage%20prix.pdf)

## B. Devices
1. **Browser APIs** (MDN compatibility data): WebUSB on desktop Chrome and Android Chrome, not in Android WebView; Web
   Serial on Android since Chrome 138 for Bluetooth RFCOMM only; Web Bluetooth is BLE only. WebUSB does not block printer
   class 0x07; on Windows the interface needs the WinUSB driver. Direct Sockets (raw TCP) only in Isolated Web Apps on
   managed ChromeOS. Local Network Access: Chrome 142 asks the user's permission for requests to the local network; HTTP
   requests from HTTPS pages to private IP literals or `.local` names are exempt from mixed-content blocking; WebSockets are
   not yet covered.
   [Chrome 138](https://developer.chrome.com/release-notes/138) · [WebUSB](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/capabilities/build-for-webusb) · [Direct Sockets](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/iwa/direct-sockets) · [LNA](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/local-network-access)
2. **Printers.** Epson TM-T20III: USB+Serial or Ethernet (+ optional Wi-Fi kit), 6-pin drawer port (24 V, pins 2 and 5),
   ESC/POS, ePOS-Print XML on Ethernet; drawer command ESC p. Prices seen: 1,959 MAD USB (iris.ma), 2,140 MAD Ethernet
   (jeshop.ma, Dec 2024). Xprinter XP-V320N/V330N: USB/LAN/Serial, 24 V drawer, ESC/POS compatible.
   [Epson TRG](https://files.support.epson.com/pdf/pos/bulk/tm-t20iii_trg_en_reva.pdf) · [ESC p](https://download4.epson.biz/sec_pubs/pos/reference_en/escpos/esc_lp.html) · [iris.ma](https://www.iris.ma/11608-imprimante-de-tickets-pos-epson-tm-t20iii-011-usb-c31ch51011.html) · [jeshop.ma](https://www.jeshop.ma/imprimante-thermique-de-tickets-pos-epson-tm-t20iii-c31ch51012/)
3. **Sunmi / iMin.** Sunmi prints through a virtual Bluetooth "InnerPrinter", an AIDL service or a JS bridge; community
   Capacitor plugins exist for Sunmi and iMin (versions for Capacitor 6–8, 2024–2026).
   [Sunmi](https://docs.sunmi.com/en/general-function-modules/printing-service/)
4. **NVR POS overlay.** Hikvision NVRs (DS-7716NXI manual, chapter 15, pages 139–141): POS protocols Universal
   Protocol, EPSON, AVE and NUCLEUS (NUCLEUS for RS-232); connection by TCP, UDP, multicast, RS-232, USB-to-RS-232 or
   sniffing; for TCP and UDP a port from 1 to 65535, unique per POS, and an "allowed remote IP address"; overlay text in
   Latin-1 only, scrolling or page mode, display time and POS event timeout 5–3600 s, privacy masking. The Universal
   Protocol's own fields (start and end markers) were not on these pages: confirm on a real NVR. Dahua has a POS
   section; details not confirmed. [p. 139](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1795534/Hikvision-Ds-7716nxi-I4-16p-4s.html?page=139) ·
   [p. 140](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1795534/Hikvision-Ds-7716nxi-I4-16p-4s.html?page=140) ·
   [p. 141](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1795534/Hikvision-Ds-7716nxi-I4-16p-4s.html?page=141)
5. **ZKTeco ADMS.** The clock is an HTTP client: `/iclock/cdata` (handshake and ATTLOG upload), `/iclock/getrequest`,
   `/iclock/devicecmd`; ATTLOG lines: PIN, time, status, verify mode, work code. Market share in Morocco not found.
   [zkteco-adms](https://github.com/s0x90/zkteco-adms)
6. **Libraries (npm, Sep 2026).** electron 44.4.5 · better-sqlite3 13.0.3 (Node 22+) · node-thermal-printer 4.6.1 (TCP and
   COM; its system-printer route depends on stale packages) · usb 3.1.0 · serialport 13.0.0 (Dec 2024) · @capacitor/core
   8.5.2 · @capacitor-community/sqlite 8.1.1. `escpos` (2020) and `@node-escpos/core` (2024) are stale — the kit has its
   own encoder.

## C. Platforms
1. **Durable Objects (SQLite).** Available on Workers Free; 10 GB per object; Free 100k requests/day and 5 GB total;
   Paid: 1 M requests/month then $0.15/M, 400k GB-s then $12.50/M GB-s, rows read 25 bn/month, rows written 50 M/month
   then $1/M, storage 5 GB-month then $0.20/GB-month; WebSocket hibernation bills no duration. EU jurisdiction:
   `namespace.jurisdiction("eu")`. Point-in-time recovery within 30 days (`getBookmarkForTime`,
   `onNextSessionRestoreBookmark`). Workers Paid $5/month includes 10 M requests and 30 M CPU-ms. The `transactionSync`
   callback "must complete synchronously" (no `async`, no Promise), so async WebCrypto hashing happens before it. WebCrypto
   PBKDF2 on Workers accepts at most 100,000 iterations (workerd issue 1346, open on 25 Sep 2026). D1 databases can be
   created in the EU jurisdiction (`wrangler d1 create … --jurisdiction eu`, changelog of 5 Nov 2025; fixed at creation).
   R2 buckets in a jurisdiction are created from the dashboard ("Specify jurisdiction") or the S3 endpoint, and the Worker
   binding must name the jurisdiction.
   [transactionSync](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/api/sqlite-storage-api/) · [PBKDF2 limit](https://github.com/cloudflare/workerd/issues/1346) · [D1 jurisdiction](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2025-11-05-d1-jurisdiction/) · [R2 data location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/reference/data-location/)
   [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/) · [limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/limits/) · [location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/reference/data-location/) · [SQLite API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/api/sqlite-storage-api/) · [Workers pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/)
2. **D1.** Free 10 databases × 500 MB; Paid 50,000 × 10 GB, 25 bn rows read and 50 M written per month included.
   [D1 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/)
3. **Supabase.** Two free projects across all organisations you own; paused after a week of inactivity; Pro from
   $25/month with $10 compute credit. (Not used by these products.) [pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing)
4. **WhatsApp Cloud API.** Per-message pricing since 1 Jul 2025; from 1 Oct 2026 Morocco gets its own rate card and
   service and utility messages are charged; reseller figures for Morocco: utility $0.0230, marketing $0.0414 (YCloud,
   4 Sep 2026) — **not confirmed on Meta's page**. [Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing) · [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-api-message-pricing-update-effective-october-1-2026)
5. **Glovo.** Partner API = Orders API + Stock & Price API; staging token, menu upload, tests, then production; the
   restaurant must be a Glovo partner; a Moroccan vendor reports 4–8 weeks of commercial approval per platform. Glovo's
   page (25 Sep 2026): orders by webhook; statuses ACCEPTED and READY_FOR_PICKUP; the first price and stock update must
   be pushed before going live; single-product updates limited to 120 calls a minute per store; one production token
   for all stores. An integrator (HubRise) lists Accepted, Ready_for_pickup, Out_for_delivery, Picked_up_by_customer;
   customer name and address with coordinates on every order, the phone only for orders the restaurant delivers;
   cancellation only through Glovo's support, which notifies the partner. Another Glovo API document (v2, "chains") uses
   different paths and statuses: build against the version Glovo gives the client.
   [Glovo](https://qcommerce-integrations.glovoapp.com/this-is-api-detail-page/) · [HubRise](https://www.hubrise.com/apps/glovo/receive-orders) ·
   [v2 document](https://partner-api-docs-tmp.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/redoc-glovo.html)
6. **Card payments.** CMI "Tap to mobile" (Sept 2024) is an app; no public API or SDK found for CMI or NAPS terminals.
   [CMI](https://www.cmi.co.ma/fr/nos-actualites/soft-pos-le-cmi-revolutionne-le-paiement-au-maroc-avec-tap-mobile)
7. **Ed25519 in browsers.** Supported in Chrome's Web Crypto since 2025 ([Chrome 137 notes](https://developer.chrome.com/release-notes/137),
   [Igalia](https://blogs.igalia.com/jfernandez/2025/08/25/ed25519-support-lands-in-chrome-what-it-means-for-developers-and-the-web/));
   Node 22 and Workers support it (tested in this pack).
8. **Time zone.** Node 22's time-zone data (2025c) moves `Africa/Casablanca` from UTC+1 to UTC+0 on 7 Feb 2027 at 03:00
   local time and back to UTC+1 on 14 Mar 2027 at 02:00 local time (computed with `packages/kit/src/timezone.js`); browsers
   and Workers use their own copy of the same database, so the kit never hard-codes an offset.
