# Moroccan Café Innovation Map (5 Sep 2026) — the twelve features

Summary of the BrandHub artifact "Moroccan Café Innovation Map" (~150 sources). Facts it cites: 63 % of commercial
transactions in cash; a pooled tip fund ("البورصط") in 100 % of cafés of a 250-worker study; coffee +25 % on 1 Apr 2025
(120→150, 160→200, 200→250 DH/kg); CNSS fines (39 %) and tax revisions (23 %) behind 16,413 closures in three regions since
Covid; Glovo commission capped at 30 %; World Cup 2026 nights with 50 DH advances and 200 DH minimums; Ramadan starting
about 8 Feb 2027.

Its funnel idea (to decide at Gate 2): a free "Kredi + Borsat + Compteur" app in Darija and French that brings cafés in
before they buy the till.

Interview questions it asked (still useful for pilots): who holds the cash during service · how tips are pooled and split
· whether the owner counts doses · tabs · what happened on World Cup nights · what the accountant receives · the last
internet or power failure · the last coffee price increase · would you pay 199 DH/month for the evening report and dose
variance · who installs and who answers on Saturday at 21:00.
