# Market scan — café and restaurant software in Morocco (pages opened 24 Sep 2026)

"n/s" = not stated on the page opened. "Not confirmed" = the page could not be opened or only a competitor says it.

## 1. CLYO Systems (the reference)
- **Company.** Sud Informatique Services SAS (SIREN 489 418 053), created 2006, Cagnes-sur-Mer and Nice, 6–9 employees in
  2023 ([Pappers](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/sud-informatique-services-489418053)). Claims "+20 ans", "+18 000
  restaurants", "+400 fonctionnalités" ([home](https://www.clyosystems.com/)). Resellers in France, Belgium, Switzerland and
  "pays francophones comme le Maroc" ([revendeurs](https://www.clyosystems.com/informations/nos-revendeurs/)).
- **Features** ([fonctionnalités](https://www.clyosystems.com/fonctionnalites/)): till, smartphone or tablet order entry; 4
  sale modes; virtual floor plan (reminders, bill requested, time, reserved); set menus, kitchen messages; live stock on
  buttons; promotions; reservations and online booking widget; "Encaissements simultanés avec plusieurs vendeurs (Vendeur
  flottant)"; hold and resume; connected scale; kiosk. Kitchen: printing by station, chef summary printer, courses across
  printers, "order ready" notifications, KDS by station ([KDS](https://www.clyosystems.com/l-ecran-cuisine-kds-de-preparation-pour-restaurant/)).
  Payment: split by item/cover/pro rata, vouchers, customer accounts invoiced monthly. Closure: automatic Z, report by
  e-mail, cash count, fiscal archive, cloud backup; accounting exports (Sage, Cegid, …). Staff: time clock, breaks,
  hourly cost, Dallas key. Stock: multi-supplier orders, zones, recipes, weighted average cost. Loyalty, SMS/e-mail
  campaigns. Online ordering "EatSelf" (free to €79/month). Delivery with routes and driver tracking; missed-call SMS.
- **Architecture.** Windows 7–11 till software ([download](https://www.clyosystems.com/telecharger-le-logiciel-de-caisse/)).
  Multi-site "CentraleSrv": each store keeps a local database and pushes every 30 s; head office needs a fixed IP, port
  forwarding 2618→3306 and .NET 4.51 ([help](https://help.clyosystems.com/hc/fr/articles/24535853426321-Configuration-de-la-centralisation-multi-etablissement));
  back office by VPN. Android waiter app "CLYO Mobility" takes orders "sans Wifi" ([PAD](https://www.clyosystems.com/application-de-prise-de-commande-sur-pad-pour-restaurant/)),
  4.6★ and 10k+ downloads on Google Play ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.clyo.caisse.app&hl=en_US)).
- **Prices.** France: from €49/month (Caisse), Gold from €129/month, add-ons €20–49/month, 1-year tacit renewal with
  3-month notice ([tarifs](https://www.clyosystems.com/tarifs/), [CGV](https://www.clyosystems.com/informations/conditions-generales/)).
  Morocco (Omnigo, Agadir, "mars 2026"): one-off licence from 4,449 MAD (Standard), 6,999 MAD (PME), 7,449 MAD (Pro, with
  "Vidéosurveillance & contrôle visuel"), installation and support included ([Omnigo](https://omnigopos.com/logiciel-caisse-maroc/)).
- **Morocco gaps.** Arabic interface claimed by the reseller only; right-to-left not documented; integrations are French
  (Deliveroo, Uber Eats, French payment stacks); nothing documented for Glovo, Yassir, CMI or Maroc Pay. NF525 certified
  (a French scheme). Reviews: easy to learn and robust hardware (independant.io), but quote-only pricing, bugs and paid
  support (Capterra, one review).

## 3. Around the till
Glovo: 30 % commission cap and no exclusivity after the July 2025 settlement ([Médias24](https://medias24.com/2025/07/31/concurrence-le-conseil-devoile-les-details-de-laccord-transactionnel-avec-glovo)); Yassir, Kooul
(launched July 2024), Done (super-app, 14 cities) deliver; Jumia Food closed end of 2023; Kaalix 3.2★, 2026 activity not
confirmed. QR menus: Mymenu from 149 DH/month. Reservations: Cadence 1,990/3,999 DH/month with CMI deposits
([cadence](https://cadencemaroc.com/blog/plateforme-reservation-restaurant-maroc-guide)). Loyalty: Carte Fidélité Maroc
(free to 300 clients), Fidelix 397 MAD/month. Credit books: Karny (Chari), Karnet, free. Payments: CMI "Tap to mobile"
(Sept 2024), Maroc Pay QR (0.5–1.5 % per Chari), NAPS/TKpay, Chari Money; acquiring open to new players since 1 May 2025;
interchange cut to 0.50 % (0.15 % small retailers) from 1 Oct 2026.

## 4. What owners complain about
Cash leaks ("entre 2 000 et 4 000 DH par mois sur chaque point de vente", a vendor's client:
[Oasis](https://oasistechnocloud.com/blog/logiciel-gestion-restaurant-maroc/)); 12 DH bills settled in about 3 seconds at
rush hour; milk, beans and mint running out; unknown margins; delivery chaos (tablets, re-keying, gaps with Glovo);
dislike of subscriptions and fear of hardware failure; card fees and tax exposure; ~20 % no-shows on reservations;
quote-only pricing and tacit renewals; bugs and paid support; compliance fear used as a sales argument.

## 5. Gaps taken by BrandHub
Offline-first Android with real Arabic at café prices · per-waiter cash and loss control read every evening · one
delivery inbox net of commission (Resto) · Moroccan tenders and customer credit with WhatsApp reminders · published MAD
prices, month to month · honest compliance (tamper-evident journal, B2B invoices, no certification claim) · cheap
reservations with deposits · native stamp card · Darija support on WhatsApp · caller ID for phone orders (later).
